# **Customer Financial Complaints - October 2025 DataDNA Challenge**

## **Objective**
To create an analytical report that identifies patterns in complaints, evaluates company responses, and highlights areas of concern for regulators and consumers.

This interactive dashboard analyzes over hundreds of customer financial complaints across US states, companies & products from May 2017- August 2023.

## **🔍 What this dashboard includes:**
- Complaint overview
- Company level insights 
- Product & Issue analysis
- Geographic insights (States & Regions)

## **📊 Key Insights**
- Total Complaints = 62516 | Timely Response: 93.8% complaints | Closed Complaints: 61022 (97.6%) | Active Complaints: 1494 | 
- Total Companies Reported: 1081 | Avg. Reputation Score: 75.1 | Avg. Response Time: 15 days | Avg. complaint/company: 58 |
- Customer complaints shows a clear upward trend over time with noticeable spike in specific months.
- Checking/saving account and Credit card/prepaid card product drives the most issues nearly 66%. While student loan had the least.
- Average response time is between 11-18 days.
- Top state share: California (CA) 21.9%. California, Florida, Texas and New York dominate the complaint landscape of ~ 47%, while North Dakota and Wyoming had the least. South and West regions lead in overall complaint volume.
- Majority of the complaints come via Web & Referral, followed by phone and postal mail.
