# **📊E-Commerce Analytics - November 2025 DataDNA Challenge**

## **Objective**
To identify loyal customers (repeat buyers) and to determine which channels and promotional campaigns drive their repeat purchases.

## **Dataset Overview**
This dataset has 3 primary table:
1. Events
2. Products
3. Customers

--- 

#  📏**DAX Measures**
- Total Revenue (Current Year, Previous Year, YoY%)
- Total Events (Current Year, Previous Year, YoY%)
- Total Quantity (Current Year, Previous Year, YoY%)
- Total Customers (Current Year, Previous Year, YoY%)
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Discount(%)

## 📝**Page 1: Executive Summary**

### **KPIs**
- Revenue = CY: $17.27M | PY: $14.57M | YoY%: +18.5%
- Events = CY: 25,704 | PY: 22,296 | YoY%: +15.3%
- Quantity = CY: 154,423 | PY: 134,851 | YoY%: +14.5%
- Customers = CY: 3,991 | PY: 3,983 | YoY%: +0.2%

### **Insights**
**1. Revenue by Year**
   - 2025: $17.27M | 2024: $14.57M

**2. Revenue Breakdown**
   - Order: $22.45M (70.54%) | Invoice: $9.38M (29.46%)
   - Majority of the revenue is collected through direct orders.

**3. Revenue by Month**
   - Highest Revenue = May to September (especially in July)
   - Lowest Revenue = January, February, November and December

**4. Revenue by Country**
   - Europe generates highest revenue, NA is the second largest region with revenue, while APAC and LATAM contribute smaller portions. 

**5. Top Performing Products**
   - Top Vendor:
        ~ Microsoft
   - Top Products include:
        ~ Microsoft 365 Business Standard Annual (~$1.63M)
        ~ Azure AI Studio Annual Standard ($942K)
        ~ Azure AI Studio Annual Pro ($935K)
        ~ Azure AI Studio Annual ($913K)
